Future Compliance Review Checklist
Document ID: NES-COMP-020 | Version: 1.0
Build approach: Cline-assisted development  Local-first Windows application
Scope: Personal research, paper trading and insurance document assistance
Planning gate before any customer-facing regulated service
1. Purpose
This is a future planning checklist, not legal advice or a determination that any activity is permitted. Obtain qualified
India-specific regulatory advice before offering regulated services to customers.
2. Trigger for review
  Providing investment advice or personalised recommendations to customers.
  Managing customer portfolios or executing trades on their behalf.
  Brokerage, order routing or customer trading access.
  Distributing, recommending, selling or purchasing insurance for customers.
  Collecting customer financial, identity or policy documents.
  Charging for financial/insurance services or making performance claims.
 
3. Review topics
  Applicable SEBI registrations/rules for the exact proposed activity.
  Broker/exchange permissions, API terms and market-data licensing.
