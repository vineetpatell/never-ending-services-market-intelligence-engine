3. Acceptance checklist
  All six primary navigation destinations work.
  No button looks active if it has no meaningful action.
  All sample data is labelled illustrative/demo.
  Dark trading and light insurance themes remain readable.
  All 12 animations replay and reduced motion is respected.
  Core UI works without WebGL.
  Paper-trading action never calls a broker or creates a real order.
  Policy extraction requires review before verified status.
  Backtest report includes period, data source, fees/slippage assumptions and limitations.
  Provider failure, stale data and missing data are clearly distinguished.
  Backup restore is tested before storing real policy documents.
 
4. Financial calculation tests
  Unit tests for indicator calculations and edge cases.
  Tests for missing bars, duplicate timestamps, split/corporate-action assumptions where supported.
  Backtest fees, slippage, trade timing and benchmark methodology tested against known examples.
  No look-ahead bias or accidental future data leakage.
  Risk metrics validated with small manually checkable datasets.
 
5. Document and AI tests
  Test PDFs with selectable text, scanned pages, malformed files and mixed layouts.
  Test incorrect/missing dates, policy numbers, premiums and exclusions.
  Ensure extracted fields preserve source location or provenance when available.
  Test Copilot questions with missing evidence and conflicting documents.
  Ensure answers admit uncertainty and do not invent insurer terms or market facts.
 
6. Release gates
 Do not connect real market data, use real personal documents or rely on AI outputs until the relevant domain tests
and privacy controls pass.
