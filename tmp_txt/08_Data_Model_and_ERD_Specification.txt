Build approach: Cline-assisted development  Local-first Windows application
Scope: Personal research, paper trading and insurance document assistance
Proposed entities, relationships and integrity rules
1. Modelling principles
  Separate source data, calculated results and user-confirmed records.
  Record timestamps and provenance for market data and extracted policy fields.
  Use stable identifiers; avoid using display names as primary keys.
  Protect sensitive fields and avoid storing unnecessary personal information.
  Version backtest configurations and model/strategy parameters.
 
2. Proposed entities

symbol, exchange, timestamp, OHLCV or quote fields, source, quality/status.

3. Relationships
  Watchlist has many WatchlistItems.
  Strategy has many versioned BacktestRuns.
  PaperPortfolio has PaperOrders and PaperPositions.
  Policy may have multiple PolicyDocuments.
  PolicyDocument has many ExtractedFields, each with review status and provenance.
  PolicyComparison references two or more Policies.
  Reminder links to a policy or other supported record.
 
4. Data integrity
  Use decimal-safe representation for money where appropriate; avoid binary floating point for persisted currency
 calculations.
 Use timezone-aware timestamps internally and display local time consistently.
  Validate quantity, date ranges, currency and category fields.
  Do not treat unverified OCR values as authoritative.
  Document migration strategy and test rollback/restore where supported.
 
5. ERD and implementation note
Create an ERD before implementing the real database. The entities above are a proposed logical model, not a final schema.
Confirm provider requirements, document retention and actual workflows before migration scripts are written.
