1. Purpose
Define how the platform reads, reviews and compares policy documents while preserving traceability and uncertainty.
2. Supported categories
  Term life insurance.
  Health insurance.
  Vehicle insurance for car/two-wheeler.
  Other categories remain out of scope until a category-specific field schema is defined.
 
3. Document processing workflow
File selected → type/size validation → text extraction/OCR → extracted fields with provenance → uncertainty flags → user
review/edit → verified record → comparison/reminders.

Field group
Examples / rules
Identity
Insurer, policy category, policy reference; mark missing or ambiguous values.
Dates
Start, expiry/renewal, waiting period dates; preserve source and timezone/date format.
Premium
Amount, currency, payment frequency, taxes if explicitly stated.
Coverage
Sum insured, covered members, benefits, limits and add-ons as applicable.
Restrictions
Exclusions, co-pay, deductible, waiting periods, sub-limits and conditions where present.
Provenance
Document name/page/section or extraction location when available.
Review
Unreviewed, corrected, verified by user, uncertain or failed.
5. Extraction rules
  Never silently convert OCR text into verified policy facts.
  Allow user correction and preserve the distinction between extracted and confirmed values.
  Do not infer an exclusion or benefit when it is not present in the source.
  Show unreadable or missing fields as unknown, not zero or “not covered”.
  Keep the source document available for verification where lawful and appropriate.
 
6. Comparison rules
  Compare like-for-like fields only and label non-comparable terms.
  Surface exclusions, restrictions, co-pay, deductibles, waiting periods and missing information—not only premiums.
  Show source document and date for each material comparison field.
  Do not create a single suitability score without transparent criteria and limitations.
  Do not invent insurer quotes, current prices or claim approval probabilities.
 
7. Claims assistance
  Provide a document checklist and relevant policy references.
  Clearly distinguish extracted policy clauses from general guidance.
  Do not guarantee claim acceptance or invent insurer-specific processes.
  Ask the user to verify deadlines and requirements with the insurer where source evidence is incomplete.
 
8. Privacy
  Use synthetic policies in the prototype.
  Keep local document storage as default in the intended application.
  Do not include full policy text or personal identifiers in logs.
  Make export, deletion and backup implications clear.
