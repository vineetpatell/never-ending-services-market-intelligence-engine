3. Status tracking
For each document, track: Draft → Reviewed → Accepted → Updated. Record owner, version, last updated date and links
to relevant code/decisions. Keep all documents in the repository under docs/.
These documents are initial project baselines. Update them as the implementation evolves; they are not evidence
that the described features are already built.
