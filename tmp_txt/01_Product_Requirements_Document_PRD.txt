Goals, scope, workflows and acceptance criteria
1. Product Summary
NES AI Finance & Insurance Intelligence Platform is a local-first personal workspace for equity-market research, historical
strategy testing, simulated paper trading, insurance-document understanding, policy comparison and AI-assisted
explanations.
Problem and goals
  Bring trading research and personal insurance records into one coherent interface.
  Help non-technical users complete guided tasks without needing to understand every technical metric.
  Keep personal documents and local AI workflows on the user's machine by default.
  Provide traceable outputs with data source, freshness, assumptions and uncertainty.
  Build incrementally, validating the frontend before connecting real data sources.
 
In scope — initial release
  Responsive dashboard and navigation; hybrid light/dark themes.
  NSE/BSE equity research, watchlists, stock screener, indicators and historical backtesting, subject to authorised data
 availability.
 Paper trading only, with explicit simulation status.

  Guaranteed predictions, invented market values, or unverified insurer quotes.
  Sending personal documents to external AI services without explicit disclosure and consent.

Non-functional requirements
  Usable without WebGL or animations.
  Accessible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.
  No API secrets in frontend bundles or normal logs.
  Clear loading, empty, success, error and retry states.
  Local-first storage defaults and tested backup/restore.
  Performance measured on target PC and a lower-power fallback device.
 
Release acceptance
  No dead navigation or misleading demo data.
  No live orders or insurance purchase flow.
  Important financial calculations have tests and documented assumptions.
  Document extraction is not treated as verified until reviewed.
  All major workflows include source/freshness/limitations where relevant.
