Build approach: Cline-assisted development  Local-first Windows application
Scope: Personal research, paper trading and insurance document assistance
Navigation, tokens, responsive behaviour and page states
1. Experience principles
  Goal-first rather than feature-first: users start with tasks such as analyse a stock or compare policies.
  Progressive disclosure: simple view first; advanced controls are optional.
  Hybrid theme: light dashboard/insurance and optional dark trading workspace.
  Explainable outputs: source, freshness, assumptions and limitations are visible.
  Motion is purposeful and can be reduced or disabled.

Typography and spacing
  Font: Inter. Page titles 28–32 px; section headings 20–24 px; body 14–16 px; metadata 12–13 px.
  Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48 px.
  Cards: 14–18 px radius; controls 40–44 px high; use restrained shadows.
  Use tabular numerals for financial values and never rely on colour alone.

4. Page wireframes and behaviour
Home
Top: global search, data status, notifications and profile. Main: greeting and goal-first command area. Quick actions for
stock analysis, screener, backtest, understand policy, compare policies and renewal review. Then demo market snapshot,
watchlist, insurance summary, tasks and recent insights.
Trading
  Market overview: session status, index summaries, chart, movers, events and source timestamp.
  Screener: simple/advanced filters, search, sorting, saved screens and demo data labels.
  Stock analysis: overview, chart, fundamentals, technicals, events and AI explanation.
  Backtesting wizard: strategy → universe → period → costs/slippage → validate → results.
  Paper trading: simulated positions and orders, with a persistent no-real-orders banner.
  Portfolio/risk: exposure, concentration and explained metrics.
 
Insurance
  Overview: policies, renewals, missing information and recent comparisons.
  Term life, health and vehicle pages show category-specific fields where available.
  Vault: upload, process status, field review, verification, search and delete/archive.
  Compare: side-by-side coverage, exclusions, premium, restrictions, source and missing data.
  Claims: policy-linked checklist; no promise of approval. Renewals: verified dates and reminder controls.
 
Copilot, Library and Settings
Copilot answers should separate extracted facts, calculations, model outputs and general guidance. Library stores saved
research and documents with source/date metadata. Settings control themes, reduced motion, data sources, local AI,
backups, privacy and diagnostics.

5. Responsive behaviour
 Viewport
Behaviour
Desktop ≥1280 px
Expanded sidebar and multi-column cards.
Tablet 768–1279 px
Collapsible sidebar, two-column cards, secondary panels as drawers.
Mobile <768 px
Drawer navigation, stacked cards, simplified chart controls and step-by-step forms.
6. Accessibility and states
  All controls have labels and keyboard focus.
  Every async component defines loading, empty, success, error and disabled states.
  Reduced-motion setting and static fallbacks are mandatory.
  Dialogs and destructive actions require clear confirmation.
  Tables remain readable and support horizontal scroll or card conversion on mobile.
