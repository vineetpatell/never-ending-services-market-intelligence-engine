How to navigate the prototype and understand its limitations
1. Start here
The prototype uses illustrative data. Until real integrations are implemented and verified, values are for interface
demonstration only.
2. Home dashboard
  Use the command area to start a task.
  Open Trading for stock research and simulated strategy workflows.
  Open Insurance to review sample policy screens and comparison behaviour.
  Use Library to find saved demo research and reports.
  Check data status badges before interpreting any displayed value.
 
3. Trading workflows
Analyse a stock
  Search a demo symbol.
  Open Stock Analysis.

Backtest a strategy

Paper trading
  Open Paper Trading.
  Enter a simulated order in the demo interface.
  Review quantity/exposure and confirm the simulation.
  Verify the persistent simulation-only banner. No real order is sent.
 
4. Insurance workflows
  Use sample policy cards to explore category pages.
  Open Policy Vault to see the prototype upload/review flow.
  Review extracted fields before treating them as verified.
  Select policies in Compare Policies to inspect differences and missing fields.
  Use Claims Assistant as a checklist organiser, not a guarantee of claim approval.
 
5. AI Copilot
  Use preset prompts to see demonstration responses.
  Check whether a response is demo content or grounded in real configured sources.
  Review cited sources and limitations when available.
  Do not enter real sensitive policy information into a prototype that has not been reviewed for privacy.
 
6. Theme and accessibility
  Switch between light dashboard and dark trading workspace.
  Enable reduced motion if animations are uncomfortable or distracting.
  Use keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
 
7. Known limitations
  Demo data is not live market data or a real insurer quote.
  No live broker orders or insurance purchases.
  Backend integrations may not yet exist.
  Prototype AI responses do not prove that a local model has been connected.
